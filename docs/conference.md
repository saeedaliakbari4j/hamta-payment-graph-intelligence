## Submission rules

- Persian or English; original, not published or under review elsewhere.
- Max 6 pages (up to 2 extra pages for a fee), two-column template from the site, Word + PDF.
- Persian template: B Mitra 11 body, abstract ≤ 200 words, 5–10 keywords; sections: abstract,
  keywords, introduction, body, conclusion, references.
- English papers → IEEE Xplore. Persian papers → ISC and Civilica. Selected papers → journal special issue.
- Generative-AI declaration form is **mandatory at submission** (before final submit in the panel).
  AI use is allowed for writing, editing, analysis, coding — must be disclosed. Fabricated data or
  citations → rejection.
- One author must register and present. Fee with paper: 17.5M rial (general), 12.5M rial (student).

## Relevant tracks

1. **Track 5 — Knowledge Management & Data Governance in the Age of AI**
   → *Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Decision Support* (best fit)
2. Track 9 — EA in Specialized Industries (payments / fintech)
3. Track 3 — Intelligent EA (AI-Augmented EA)

To fit an EA conference, frame the work as an intelligent decision-support capability inside a
payment company's architecture (data → governance/anonymization → graph analytics → model →
campaign/CRM), not only as a model.
